# Portfolio / Creative Showcase Pattern

## Goal
Make the work and creative point of view memorable without sacrificing navigation.

## Art direction
Choose an intentional visual concept: editorial, brutalist, cinematic, minimal, spatial, tactile, typographic, etc. Do not combine trends randomly.

## Projects
Show outcomes and context. Let imagery/video breathe. Project transitions can carry identity.

## Motion
Level 2–3 is appropriate when purposeful. Use choreography, 3D, magnetic or spatial effects selectively around hero/project presentation.

## Usability
Navigation, text readability, reduced motion and mobile alternatives remain mandatory.

## Avoid
Effects with no relationship to the work, cursor gimmicks everywhere, unreadable experimental type, long intro sequences users cannot bypass.
