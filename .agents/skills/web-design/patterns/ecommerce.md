# E-commerce Pattern

## Goal
Support discovery, evaluation, confidence and purchase without ambiguity.

## Discovery
Search, categories and filters reflect catalog complexity. Show active filters and easy reset.

## Product
Prioritize imagery, title, price, availability, variants, delivery/returns context and primary purchase action. Variant state must be unmistakable.

## Cart/checkout
Preserve choices, expose costs before commitment, prevent accidental duplicate actions, and clearly communicate errors/recovery.

## Motion
Level 1–2. Product media transitions may be expressive but must not interfere with evaluation.

## Avoid
Hidden total costs, ambiguous availability, disabled purchase action with no explanation, excessive urgency patterns, animation that obscures product information.
