# Elevation and Radius

Shape and elevation express role.

## Radius
Use tighter radii for compact utility controls, moderate radii for panels/cards, larger radii for floating transient surfaces. Pills require semantic or compositional reason.

## Elevation
- Level 0: base/work surface
- Level 1: grouped/raised content
- Level 2: floating toolbar/popover
- Level 3: modal/critical transient layer

Use borders for adjacent-surface separation and shadows for actual depth. Translucent surfaces may combine edge highlight and restrained shadow.

Do not make every component equally rounded or elevated. Hierarchy disappears when everything floats.
