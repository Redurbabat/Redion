# Direct Scrub Selection

Some adjacent choice groups can allow a pointer/finger to press and scrub continuously across options while one shared selection material follows.

Best for reversible, low-cost mode choices such as segmented controls or tool variants. Usually inappropriate for navigation that triggers expensive loads or irreversible actions.

## Gesture
Capture the pointer after intentional activation. Track position against stable option hit regions. Preview the candidate continuously; commit according to the component contract, commonly on release.

Allow cancellation by leaving a tolerance region or Escape where applicable. Do not trigger every intermediate option's expensive side effects while scrubbing.

Stylus and touch may use different slop thresholds. Mouse drag should not interfere with text selection outside the component.

Reduced motion keeps direct hit feedback but removes interpolated material travel.
