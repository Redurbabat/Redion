# Responsive Motion Choreography

Motion adapts with layout.

Desktop may use pointer proximity, hover, side-panel travel and spatial multi-pane transitions. Tablet prioritizes touch/stylus continuity and window-size adaptation. Phone reduces travel distances and favors sheets, stack navigation and reachable controls.

When layout crosses a breakpoint, do not animate every reflowed element. Preserve stable anchors and animate only relationships users need to understand.

Orientation, split-view and window resizing must not trigger theatrical page entrances. Reduced motion and low-power conditions simplify choreography further.
