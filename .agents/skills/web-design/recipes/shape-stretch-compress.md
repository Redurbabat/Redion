# Shape Stretch and Compress

A shared selection/material surface may briefly stretch toward its destination and compress as it settles. This can strengthen spatial continuity between nearby related targets.

Keep deformation subtle. Preserve recognizable control boundaries and avoid cartoon-like elasticity in professional/productivity UI.

## Geometry
Stretch along the travel axis, not arbitrarily. Couple deformation to distance/velocity and decay it as the surface approaches its target. Radius can adapt continuously but must not invert or create sharp transient corners.

Do not scale labels/icons with the material blob; content should remain optically stable.

Disable deformation for long-distance navigation, unrelated controls and reduced motion.
