# Platform Consistency Review

Before completion, distinguish product identity from platform convention.

Verify navigation, back behavior, menus, keyboard shortcuts, sheets/popovers, windowing, touch/stylus behavior, safe areas/insets and accessibility against the target platform.

Do not copy Apple conventions onto Android/Windows by default. Do not force Material components into an established custom web product without reason. Shared brand tokens may span platforms while interaction conventions adapt.

When targeting multiple platforms, document which behaviors are shared and which are platform-specific.
