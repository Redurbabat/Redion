# Tooltips

Tooltips explain unfamiliar compact controls; they do not carry essential workflow content.

Desktop pointer/focus may reveal them after a short intentional delay. Keyboard focus must also expose relevant help. Touch needs another mechanism; do not depend on hover.

Keep copy concise. Position without obscuring the target or important work. Do not animate tooltips dramatically.

For icon-only toolbars, accessible names are mandatory even when a tooltip exists.
