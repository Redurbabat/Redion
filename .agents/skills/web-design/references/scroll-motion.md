# Scroll Motion

Scroll motion should reinforce narrative, hierarchy or spatial continuity without stealing scroll control.

## Allowed
- subtle reveal of supporting content
- sticky storytelling where content benefits from comparison
- progress-linked diagrams
- restrained parallax/depth
- header/tool chrome adaptation

## Avoid
- scroll hijacking
- long pinned sections with little information
- every section flying in
- large text movement that harms reading
- effects that require perfect wheel velocity

Use native scroll as the default. Progressive enhancement only. Respect reduced motion and maintain keyboard/touch scrolling.
